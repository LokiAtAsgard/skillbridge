Safety and Trust

Why Youth Trust SkillBridge Employers

Scam job postings are a real problem in the Philippines. SkillBridge addresses this by requiring all employers to go through PESO verification before any listing goes live. Youth can see your Verified badge and know the opportunity is legitimate.

  • ✓ Business registration verified through PESO
  • ✓ Listings reviewed before going live
  • ✓ Report and flag system for suspicious activity
  • ✓ Allowance and duration must follow DOLE DO 186-17
🏛

How to Get Verified

1
Register your employer account on SkillBridge
2

3

4
Post your first listing and start receiving matched applicants
